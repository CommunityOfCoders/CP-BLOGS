# Welcome to CP Club VJTI 🚀

CP Club VJTI is the competitive programming community at Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute, Mumbai.
We are passionate about algorithms, problem-solving, and nurturing the next generation of competitive programmers.

## What We Do

- 🧠 **Weekly practice sessions** : curated problem sets from Codeforces, AtCoder, and LeetCode
- 📝 **In-depth blogs** : tutorials on algorithms, data structures, and contest strategies
- 🏆 **Contest training** : preparation for ICPC regionals and national olympiads
- 👥 **Community discussions** : collaborate, debug, and grow together

## Latest Topics

From graph theory to dynamic programming, segment trees to number theory : our blogs cover a wide spectrum of competitive programming concepts, written by students who've been in the trenches.

> *"The only way to get better at competitive programming is to write more code, debug more bugs, and read more editorials."*

## Join Us

Whether you're a beginner picking up your first language or an experienced coder aiming for ICPC, CP Club VJTI has a place for you.

Browse our blogs using the navigation on the left, or use the **search bar** to find topics you care about.

Happy coding! 💻
